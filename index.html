Helping You Pray Without Shame or Confusion

Prayer Portals were born from a simple observation: many of us want to connect with God but struggle to find the words. Whether you're feeling overwhelmed, grateful, confused, or hopeful—these cards meet you where you are.

Each card connects an emotion or need with relevant Scripture and a prayer starter. They won't pray for you, but they'll help you begin. Created in Christchurch, New Zealand, for youth ministries, small groups, and anyone seeking a deeper prayer life.

"Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."

— Psalm 119:105

How Prayer Portals Work

Three simple steps to transform your prayer life

1

Identify Your Feeling

Browse the cards until you find the emotion, need, or desire that resonates with what you're experiencing right now.

2

Discover Scripture

Flip the card over to find Bible verses that speak directly to your situation, connecting God's Word to your heart.

3

Begin Your Prayer

Use the prayer starter as a launching point. Let it guide you into honest, meaningful conversation with God.
